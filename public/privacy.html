This privacy notice describes the information handled by Flowline at flowline-yash.netlify.app, operated by Yash Anand.

Information used for sign-in

When you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information with our authentication provider, Supabase. Flowline uses your verified identity, name, and email to create or reconnect your private workspace. Google sign-in does not grant Flowline access to Gmail or Google Drive.

Workspace information

Flowline stores workflow definitions, integration settings, incoming webhook data, execution inputs and outputs, errors, and run history so you can build and inspect your automations. Integration credentials are encrypted before storage. Only connect services and submit information you are authorized to use.

Where information goes

Netlify hosts the application and runs its server functions. Supabase provides authentication and PostgreSQL storage. These providers process information needed to operate the service, including technical request and security logs. Configured workflow actions send the data you select to their destination services, such as Slack or Resend. Preview mode does not deliver Slack messages or emails. Flowline does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

Cookies and account security

Flowline uses an essential session cookie to keep you signed in for up to 30 days and a short-lived cookie to complete Google sign-in. Signing out removes the current Flowline session. Signing out of Google separately does not automatically end a Flowline session. A workspace recovery key also grants access to that workspace; keep it private.

Retention and your choices

Workspace data remains available until it is deleted. You can export or delete workflows and remove integrations in the app. For account deletion or a request concerning stored personal information, contact the operator using the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen. Operational logs and backups may retain copies after data is removed from the live application. Revoking Google access stops future Google authorization but does not itself delete a Flowline workspace.

Changes

This page will be updated when the service's data practices change. The date above identifies the current notice.
